## 01 — Road Damage Detection and Human Review

建议 A · 15 秒

We study visible road damage in camera images. The project connects detection, evaluation and human review. We will explain why we chose the problem, the design goals and the workflow, then examine the evidence produced at each stage.

## 02 — Why road damage inspection?

建议 A · 35 秒

The starting point is ordinary road imagery. These supplied examples come from three countries and show different roads and capture conditions. A useful system should locate visible defects while letting a person check what the model found or missed. This motivates both road-damage detection and a review workflow, rather than a result consisting only of a model score.

## 03 — Design goals and guiding principles

建议 A · 40 秒

The project has three connected goals. First, detect visible damage with a class and location. Second, compare the available checkpoints using a common inference protocol. Third, keep enough evidence for a person to review the result. These goals determine the measurements: annotated box matches, AP and recall, timing, and concrete inspection examples. Photographs alone do not certify depth or repair urgency.

## 04 — The project workflow

建议 A · 35 秒

This workflow is the organizing structure of the project. We prepare traceable images and annotations, run two trained checkpoints, evaluate their behavior, investigate individual results, and deliver an application with measured runtime and downloadable evidence. The stages connect the model experiments to the final demonstration. New training is not part of the current interface revision.

## 05 — Three connected application functions

建议 A · 35 秒

The application has three connected areas. The case library supplies original images with source annotations. The detection workspace runs one or both checkpoints, compares their boxes and exports a record. Experiments investigate geometry, network responses and runtime for the current image. Country diagnostics instead use eighteen bundled reference cases. Historical adaptation and ONNX records are displayed separately, so they are not mistaken for experiments trained live.

## 06 — The input images contain four damage classes

建议 B · 35 秒

The dataset provides images and annotated boxes for four visible damage classes. D00 runs along the road, D10 across it, D20 forms connected cracks, and D40 denotes a pothole. These examples show the original annotation regions, not model outputs. Their role is to make the detection target clear before looking at quantitative results.

## 07 — Separate fitting data from evaluation data

建议 B · 45 秒

The source countries provide training, validation and test sets. The original US set is a separate domain test. Keeping test data out of fitting is necessary, but filename separation is not enough: the audit found similar cross-split scenes and conflicting annotations among exact duplicates. We must resolve those cases and freeze a common data version before stronger claims or controlled retraining.

## 08 — From an image to candidate damage boxes

建议 B · 40 秒

Both checkpoints detect the same four damage classes. The image is resized under the common inference setup, the network produces box candidates, and confidence filtering plus non-maximum suppression produces the displayed detections. YOLO11n is the baseline and YOLO11s the comparison. The supplied weights were trained under different budgets, which limits causal conclusions about model size.

## 09 — What do the two checkpoints detect?

建议 B · 45 秒

Under the same inference settings, the larger checkpoint obtains about 0.286 mAP50, compared with 0.209 for the baseline. Recall at the displayed confidence threshold remains low for both. AP summarizes performance over confidence thresholds; it is distinct from recall at one threshold. This compares existing trained checkpoints. Their training budgets differ, so it is not proof that architecture alone caused the gap.

## 10 — Does performance transfer to another country?

建议 C · 45 秒

The next validation question is transfer. The larger checkpoint drops from roughly 0.286 mAP50 on the source test to 0.091 on the original US test; the baseline also drops. Country changes multiple factors, so this does not identify one cause. It does show why local benchmark performance alone is insufficient for deployment in a different setting.

## 11 — Which image changes weaken detection?

建议 C · 45 秒

This recorded experiment applies controlled image changes to 350 test images. Motion blur harms overall mAP strongly, while pothole recall falls less in this sample. Fog and sensor noise also reduce performance. These are synthetic sensitivity checks with their own recorded protocol. They should not be described as a real-world weather evaluation or as proof of a particular internal mechanism.

## 12 — How are detections prioritized for review?

建议 C · 40 秒

The review layer makes its assumptions explicit. Different class weights combine with confidence, an area proxy and multiplicity. In the displayed one-box D00 example, the score is about 0.79 and the priority is LOW. These values organize review; they are not calibrated physical severity or repair deadlines. The original image and missed annotations remain important even when the score is low.

## 13 — Perspective changes the area estimate

建议 C · 45 秒

The same prediction can be investigated geometrically. The original image and assumed bird’s-eye plane show the projection. Changing the assumed width from 3.5 to 4 metres changes estimated area from 1.3771 to 1.5738 square metres, while priority remains LOW. This is a sensitivity result, not a calibrated physical measurement. The recorded forty-image study changed tiers on only two frames.

## 14 — Feature responses follow the current image

建议 D · 45 秒

The analysis reads activations from the actual prediction network at the P3, P4 and P5 detection inputs. The four images show the prediction and the corresponding response maps. The bars summarize normalized energy inside the current predicted region. Changing brightness or blur changes the input and recomputes the responses. This is not a fixed class-wide statistic or a causal explanation.

## 15 — Inspect misses on a small reference set

建议 D · 40 秒

The live country diagnostic complements the full evaluation by making failures inspectable. It runs one model on six original reference images from each country, then matches same-class boxes one-to-one at IoU at least 0.5. This run has no false-positive boxes but many missed annotations. Selecting a result links the specific image back to feature investigation. The selected set is too small to estimate country accuracy.

## 16 — Can a small US training set help?

建议 D · 45 秒

After diagnosing a country gap, a separate recorded study tests small labelled US training subsets. The one-hundred-image checkpoint improves US holdout mAP50 to 0.1957, while source mAP50 falls to 0.2198. The two-hundred-image condition loses still more source performance. Pothole recall decreases, so aggregate target AP alone is insufficient. The application still uses the original comparison checkpoints.

## 17 — Measure runtime with a defined scope

建议 D · 35 秒

Runtime needs a clear measurement scope. This recorded GPU experiment measures one hundred calls after thirty warmup calls. Pure inference and the full pipeline include different work, which explains the two means. The web lab measures the current machine separately. Neither measurement is evidence of Jetson, public-cloud or guaranteed video performance.

## 18 — Check outputs after changing model format

建议 E · 35 秒

Export is another step requiring evidence. In the recorded CPU parity test, FP32 preserved outputs within the tolerance. FP16 missed one detection and dynamic INT8 caused much larger box differences. A smaller file therefore does not establish acceptable output equivalence. These are existing export experiments; the current workbench uses the PyTorch weights and does not perform export during the demonstration.

## 19 — One image through the complete workbench

建议 E · 90 秒

Play the eighty-five-second captioned demonstration. The workflow loads one original road case, compares both checkpoints, opens the investigation, changes a geometry assumption and an image condition, inspects country results, measures runtime and keeps the JSON record. The two complete output images shown here are from an actual workspace run. An online deployment remains a separate, unverified delivery option.

## 20 — What is verified, and what remains?

建议 E · 35 秒

The final acceptance check separates working software from scientific validity. The local suite passes with one operating-system privilege skip, and actual browser and inference checks cover the main workflow. A teammate must still verify another physical machine. The data conflicts and differing training budgets also remain. We should resolve and version the data, retrain under common conditions and then repeat evaluation.

## 21 — The project connects detection and review

建议 E · 30 秒

The project follows one connected path from data to detection, validation, review and delivery. It produces a working, reviewable prototype and experimental evidence about its limits. The available comparison checkpoint scores higher on the source benchmark, but recall and country transfer remain weak. The next priority is controlled validation, rather than a stronger deployment claim.
